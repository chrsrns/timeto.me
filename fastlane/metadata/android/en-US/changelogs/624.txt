26 September 2026:

1. Alarm Mode
A timer can now end with a real alarm instead of a single notification. The alarm rings and vibrates until you snooze it or start a new activity. Snooze Duration is set in Settings, and Alarm Mode can be enabled for all activities or per activity.

2. Repeat Expired Timer
The expired timer notification can repeat at a chosen interval until you start a new activity. Set it in Settings.
